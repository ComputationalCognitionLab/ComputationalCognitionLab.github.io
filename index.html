Our Research

What we study

Curiosity

How do humans and animals explore the world and forage for learning opportunities?

Creativity

How do we recombine known elements into something genuinely new?

Complexity

How does complex cognition emerge from simple components across evolution and development?

Across Development

The secret to human uniqueness lies in our long period of development: infants and children can actively explore the world and learn many complex cognitive and social skills from others. How does this happen?

Across Species

Can we really say that something is special to humans if our closest relatives, the great apes, have it too? We compare brains and cognition across species to understand how they differ, and what makes us unique.

Across the Globe

All cultures are different. If we want to understand the cognitive abilities that are universal to all humans, we have to look for them across all cultures. Studying cultures also helps us understand how and why we vary, and which developmental and environmental factors shape those variations.

Experimental work

We use eye-tracking, pupillometry and behavioural techniques to study how cognition changes across species and development.

Computational modelling

We develop computational models and artificial neural networks to gain mechanistic insights on cognition.

Brain data

We work with brain data to ground our understanding of cognition in what is biologically plausible.

Our Values

TransparencyWe share data, code and expertise, and that's one first important aspect of transparency. But we also share ideas, voice disagreements, admit mistakes, and try to always give helpful advice: this openness, too, is foundational to us and our work.

CareIn the lab, mutual care and respect are valued. We are a team, and goals are always shared. Helping each other and making sure that we are all thriving is foundational to who we are as a team.

Slow scienceSlow does not mean idle: there is a lot to do in a research project, many aspects to think about and keep track of, but they should not be rushed. The focus is on learning and understanding, not delivering. We are the only known animal in the universe with a chance to understand where we come from and who we are. Let's take this as an exciting gift, not a stressful chore. Find out more →
